# 📚 Study Tracker — FocusFlow

> **Master Your Time, Master Your Grades.**

Study Tracker is a web-based productivity application designed to help students manage their study time, track learning sessions, stay consistent, and understand their study habits. It provides a simple, organized space to monitor daily progress and improve focus.

## 🌐 Live Demo

🔗 [Visit Study Tracker](https://studytrackerog.vercel.app/)

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication** — Login and manage your personal study space.
- ⏱️ **Study Timer** — Start, pause, reset, and track study sessions.
- 📝 **Session Logging** — Record study sessions with subjects and duration.
- 📊 **Progress Dashboard** — Visualize your study activity and progress.
- 📚 **Subject Tracking** — Organize study sessions subject-wise.
- 🤖 **AI Study Insights** — Get helpful study tips and personalized insights.
- 📈 **Study Analytics** — View charts and understand your study patterns.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Mode** — Switch themes based on your preference.
- 🎯 **Productivity Focus** — Build better study habits and consistency.
